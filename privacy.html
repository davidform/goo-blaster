Scope and summary

GOO BLASTER is developed by DemjaStudio. The game works offline and does not require an account or login. The current game does not include advertising, tracking, analytics, or a developer-operated crash-reporting service. This policy distinguishes gameplay from website hosting, platform services, and email support.

Local saves and deletion

The game stores stage progress, Candy Coins, permanent upgrades, language, a full-version unlock flag, save-format version, and whether a backup reminder has been shown. Web saves use browser localStorage. The native app uses Capacitor Preferences and localStorage. The game does not send these saves to a developer-operated server.

The Android project permits operating-system backup (allowBackup=true). Depending on your device and settings, the operating system may back up or transfer app data. These copies are governed by your platform settings and are not a developer-operated cloud-save service.

“Restart from Stage 1” resets stage progress only; it does not erase coins, upgrades, language, or the unlock flag. To remove local saves, clear the browser's site data or the app's storage using device settings. Uninstalling normally removes local app storage, but system backups may remain or restore data. Manage those copies through your platform's backup controls. Exported backup codes and images must be deleted separately.

Framework, permissions, and exports

The native app uses the third-party Capacitor framework, including Preferences for local storage and Splash Screen for the launch screen. Using these components is not the same as using an advertising or analytics SDK.

The current game does not request your name, contact details, location, camera, microphone, contacts, or advertising identifier during play. It does not scan your existing photos or files. If you choose to copy a backup code or save a game image, it writes to the clipboard or initiates an image download. Sharing those exports outside the game is your choice.

Children and purchases

The game is intended for children and does not request personal information during play. It contains no advertising, paid random loot boxes, or purchase-pressure countdowns. Free random upgrades within a game are not paid purchases. Parents or guardians should handle purchases and support requests.

The planned mobile release is a single paid download that includes the full game, without subscriptions or consumable purchases. The current build does not implement an in-app purchase checkout. Where purchases are offered, the relevant storefront processes payment under its own policies. We do not receive your full payment-card details; storefronts may provide sales and transaction reports for accounting and support.

Network and platform services

The game content is bundled locally in the app; normal gameplay does not require an internet connection. The Android project includes internet permission, which alone does not establish that data is transmitted. Website loading, following an external link, store downloads, purchases, updates, and operating-system backup may use network services independently of offline gameplay.

The web edition is hosted on GitHub Pages and itch.io. Hosting platforms may process connection information such as IP addresses and server logs when you visit. Their services, and any platform-level diagnostics or backups you enable, are governed by the respective providers rather than this game's local-save logic.

Support and retention

If you or your guardian emails davidformbz@gmail.com, we receive the sender address and information voluntarily included in the message. We use this information to respond and resolve the request. Please do not send a child's name, age, or other unnecessary personal information. Email is processed through our email provider. Support messages are retained only as needed for the request and applicable legal obligations; you may contact us to request deletion. We do not sell support information or use it for advertising.

Policy updates and contact

We will update this policy and its date when the relevant practices change. Contact DemjaStudio at davidformbz@gmail.com with privacy questions or deletion requests.

隱私權政策 —— GOO BLASTER

適用範圍與摘要

GOO BLASTER 由 DemjaStudio 開發。遊戲可離線遊玩，不需要帳號或登入。目前遊戲未包含廣告、追蹤、分析工具或由開發者營運的當機回報服務。本政策區分遊戲本身、網站託管、平台服務與電子郵件客服。

本機存檔與刪除

遊戲會儲存關卡進度、糖果幣、永久強化、語言、完整版解鎖旗標、存檔格式版本，以及是否已顯示備份提醒。網頁版使用瀏覽器 localStorage；原生 App 使用 Capacitor Preferences 與 localStorage。遊戲不會把這些存檔傳送到開發者營運的伺服器。

Android 專案允許作業系統備份（allowBackup=true）。依裝置與設定，作業系統可能備份或移轉 App 資料。這些副本由你的平台設定管理，不是開發者提供的雲端存檔服務。

「從第 1 關重來」只重設關卡進度，不會清除糖果幣、強化、語言或解鎖旗標。若要刪除本機存檔，請清除瀏覽器網站資料，或在裝置設定中清除 App 儲存資料。解除安裝通常會刪除 App 本機儲存資料，但系統備份可能仍保留或還原資料，需另透過平台的備份設定管理。已匯出的備份碼與圖片也需另行刪除。

框架、權限與匯出

原生 App 使用第三方 Capacitor 框架，包括負責本機儲存的 Preferences，以及啟動畫面的 Splash Screen。使用這些元件不等於使用廣告或分析 SDK。

目前遊戲不會在遊玩過程中要求姓名、聯絡資訊、位置、相機、麥克風、通訊錄或廣告識別碼，也不會掃描既有相片或檔案。當你主動複製備份碼或儲存遊戲圖片時，遊戲會寫入剪貼簿或啟動圖片下載。是否將匯出內容分享至遊戲之外，由你決定。

兒童與購買

本遊戲以兒童為目標玩家，遊玩時不要求個人資訊。遊戲沒有廣告、隨機付費寶箱或催促購買的倒數。局內免費隨機升級不涉及付費購買。購買及客服聯繫應由家長或監護人處理。

行動版目前規劃為一次付費下載完整遊戲，不含訂閱或消耗型購買；目前版本尚未實作 App 內購結帳。若於商店提供購買，付款由該商店依其政策處理。我們不會取得完整付款卡片資料；商店可能提供用於帳務與客服的銷售及交易報表。

網路與平台服務

App 內已包含遊戲內容，正常遊玩不需要網路。Android 專案具有網路權限，但權限本身不代表有傳輸資料。載入網站、開啟外部連結、商店下載、購買、更新及作業系統備份，可能各自使用網路服務，與離線遊戲流程不同。

網頁版託管於 GitHub Pages 與 itch.io。造訪網站時，託管平台可能處理 IP 位址及伺服器記錄等連線資訊。這些服務，以及你啟用的平台診斷或備份，適用各提供者的政策，不由遊戲的本機存檔邏輯管理。

客服與保留期限

若你或監護人寄信至 davidformbz@gmail.com，我們會收到寄件地址及信中主動提供的資訊，用於回覆與處理問題。請勿提供兒童姓名、年齡或其他不必要的個人資訊。電子郵件由我們使用的郵件服務商處理。客服信件只在處理問題與履行適用法律義務所需期間保留；你可來信要求刪除。我們不販售客服資訊，也不將其用於廣告。

政策更新與聯絡

相關做法變更時，我們會更新本政策及日期。如有隱私問題或刪除請求，請聯絡 DemjaStudio：davidformbz@gmail.com。
