# Privacy Policy — GOO BLASTER

**Last updated: 22 August 2026**

> 中文版在下方 / Chinese version below.

---

## The short version

**GOO BLASTER does not collect any personal data. None. From anyone.**

There is no account, no login, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party
SDK of any kind. The game never sends your information anywhere, because it never
gathers any information to send.

---

## What is stored, and where

The game saves your progress so you can continue later. This includes:

- Which stage you have reached
- How many Candy Coins you have earned
- Which permanent upgrades you have purchased
- Which language you selected
- Whether you have unlocked the full version

**All of this is stored only on your own device**, in your browser's local storage
or, in the app version, in the app's own private storage. It is never uploaded,
never transmitted, and never seen by us or by anyone else.

You can erase all of it at any time by using "Restart from Stage 1" inside the
game, by clearing your browser's site data, or by uninstalling the app.

---

## What we do NOT do

- ❌ We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or any contact details
- ❌ We do not collect location data
- ❌ We do not access your camera, microphone, contacts, photos, or files
- ❌ We do not use advertising identifiers, device identifiers (IMEI, MAC address,
  SIM serial), or any form of tracking
- ❌ We do not show advertisements
- ❌ We do not use analytics or crash-reporting services
- ❌ We do not use cookies for tracking
- ❌ We do not share, sell, or transfer data to anyone, because we hold none

---

## Children's privacy

GOO BLASTER is designed to be suitable for children.

Because the game collects no personal information whatsoever, it does not collect
personal information from children either. This applies regardless of the age of
the player.

The game contains **no gacha mechanics, no randomised paid loot boxes, no timed
pressure mechanics, and no advertising**. These were deliberate design decisions
made at the start of the project, not features that were removed later.

If the game offers a paid unlock, it is a **single one-time purchase** handled
entirely by Apple's App Store or Google Play. We never see or handle payment
information.

---

## Network access

The game is a single HTML file with no external dependencies. Once the page has
loaded, it works fully offline — you can switch your device to airplane mode and
keep playing.

The web version is hosted on GitHub Pages and itch.io. Those platforms may keep
their own standard server logs when you load the page; that is outside our control
and is governed by their own privacy policies:

- GitHub Pages: https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies
- itch.io: https://itch.io/docs/legal/privacy-policy

The app version makes no network requests at all.

---

## Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the "Last updated" date at the top will change with
it. Since the game collects nothing, any future change would most likely be a
clarification rather than a change in practice.

# 隱私權政策 —— GOO BLASTER

**最後更新：2026 年 8 月 22 日**

## 簡短版本

**GOO BLASTER 不收集任何個人資料。一筆都沒有。**

沒有帳號、沒有登入、沒有分析工具、沒有廣告，也沒有任何第三方 SDK。
這個遊戲不會把你的資訊送到任何地方，因為它根本沒有蒐集任何資訊。

## 遊戲儲存了什麼、存在哪裡

為了讓你之後能接著玩，遊戲會儲存以下內容：

- 你打到第幾關
- 你有多少糖果幣
- 你買了哪些永久強化
- 你選擇的語言
- 你是否已解鎖完整版

**這些全部只存在你自己的裝置上**——瀏覽器的本機儲存空間，
或是（App 版）App 自己的私有儲存空間。它們不會被上傳、不會被傳輸，
我們與任何其他人都看不到。

你隨時可以清除這些資料：使用遊戲內的「從第 1 關重來」、
清除瀏覽器的網站資料，或是解除安裝 App。

## 我們不做的事

- ❌ 不收集姓名、電子郵件、電話或任何聯絡資訊
- ❌ 不收集位置資料
- ❌ 不存取相機、麥克風、通訊錄、相片或檔案
- ❌ 不使用廣告識別碼、裝置識別碼（IMEI、MAC 位址、SIM 序號）或任何追蹤技術
- ❌ 不顯示廣告
- ❌ 不使用分析或當機回報服務
- ❌ 不使用追蹤用的 cookie
- ❌ 不分享、販售或轉移資料給任何人——因為我們手上沒有資料

## 兒童隱私

GOO BLASTER 的設計就是要適合兒童遊玩。

由於本遊戲完全不收集任何個人資訊，自然也不會收集兒童的個人資訊。
這一點不因玩家年齡而有任何差異。

本遊戲**沒有轉蛋機制、沒有隨機付費寶箱、沒有限時壓力機制、也沒有廣告**。
這些是專案一開始就做的設計決定，不是後來才拿掉的功能。

若遊戲提供付費解鎖，那是**一次性買斷**，
完全由 Apple App Store 或 Google Play 處理，我們不會接觸到任何付款資訊。

## 網路存取

本遊戲是一個沒有外部相依的單一 HTML 檔案。網頁載入完成之後即可完全離線遊玩——
你可以把裝置切到飛航模式繼續玩。

網頁版託管於 GitHub Pages 與 itch.io。當你載入頁面時，
這些平台可能會保留它們自己的標準伺服器記錄；那不在我們的控制範圍內，
並適用它們各自的隱私權政策。App 版則完全不發出任何網路請求。

## 政策變更

若本政策有任何變更，最上方的「最後更新」日期會隨之更新。
由於本遊戲不收集任何資料，未來的變更較可能是文字澄清，而非實際做法的改變。
