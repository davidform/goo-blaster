English

v0.9.55 is now available in the browser. This update brings clearer upgrade choices, a movement-based Fire Candy effect, and a rebalance of later chapter bosses.

  • Clearer level-ups: collecting a large batch of XP can legitimately earn several levels at once. The upgrade screen now shows the reached level and the remaining choices accurately, with protection against repeated clicks and rewards carrying into another run.
  • Fire Candy leaves a short-lived trail as you move. Use your route to damage ordinary enemies; overlapping flames do not multiply the damage, and collecting another Fire Candy refreshes the effect.
  • Chapter-boss health from Stage 15 onward has been reduced to better match the player's capped growth. The first two chapter bosses are unchanged. Later stages still require movement and good upgrade choices.

Please let me know which stage you reach, whether upgrade choices are clear, and how the fire trail feels on your phone. Browser checks are complete for this release, but real-device feedback is still important.

繁體中文

v0.9.55 已可在瀏覽器遊玩。本次重點是升級選擇更清楚、火焰糖加入走位留火，以及後段章節 Boss 血量調整。

  • 升級顯示：一次撿到大量經驗值，本來就可能連升數級。現在會正確顯示到達等級與剩餘選卡次數，並防止重複點擊或上一局獎勵帶進新的一局。
  • 火焰糖：移動時留下短暫火焰，用走位傷害普通敵人；火焰重疊不會加倍傷害，再撿一顆會刷新效果。
  • 後段平衡：降低第 15 關起章節 Boss 的血量，讓它更符合玩家有上限的成長。前兩個章節 Boss 不變，後段仍需要走位與升級策略。

歡迎回報目前打到哪一關、升級選擇是否清楚，以及手機上的火焰走位手感。此版本已完成瀏覽器檢查，真機體驗仍需要你的回饋。
