English

The Candy Garden now has a hands-on decorating puzzle. Place mint, flowers, ponds, benches and lanterns on a 4 by 4 garden. Decorations are free to move or return, so you can experiment without spending your seeds or petals.

Six visitors have different wishes: a flower-lined picnic seat, a cool mint pond, a lantern walk and more. The first visitor is available immediately; the others arrive as you clear Stages 2, 5, 10, 15 and 20. Fulfil a wish and welcome its resident home. They stay even when you redesign the garden.

Your crops, cottage, stage progress and purchases are preserved. Gardening still adds no combat stats and has no real-time countdown. This is a small set of six layout challenges, not an endless farming mode. Tell us which wish was enjoyable and which felt like a chore.

繁體中文

糖果庭院加入可以動手玩的佈置解謎：在4×4庭院放置薄荷、花叢、池塘、長椅與提燈。擺飾可以免費移動或收回，不消耗種子與花瓣，可以放心嘗試不同布局。

六位訪客各有願望，例如花叢旁的野餐座位、薄荷池塘與提燈小徑。第一位立即出現，後續隨通過第2、5、10、15、20關開放。完成願望就能迎接居民入住，之後重新佈置也不會讓他們離開。

原有作物、小屋、關卡進度與購買等級都保留。不增加戰鬥數值，也沒有現實時間倒數。這次是六個小型空間任務，並非無限農場模式；歡迎告訴我們哪個願望有趣、哪個像在做功課。
