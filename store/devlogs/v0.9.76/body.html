English

Tap a garden field to choose a plant right beside it. Crop icons, growing times and yields now appear together, without scrolling down to a separate form. Tap the cottage for its restoration controls, and use Cancel or tap outside to return to the scene.

The kitchen, village orders and decorating tools now have focused workspaces with a persistent category bar. Wide screens place the restoration goal beside the village. Your crops keep their timers, and your saved resources, progress and combat rules stay the same.

繁體中文

點一下田地，就能在附近選擇植物。作物圖示、成長時間與產量集中在同一張操作卡，不必再拉到下方找表單。點小屋可直接操作修復，按取消或點卡片外側即可回到場景。

廚房、村莊委託與佈置工具改成各自聚焦的工作頁，上方分類列可直接切換。寬螢幕將修復目標放在村莊旁；已種作物的倒數、存檔資源、進度與戰鬥規則維持原樣。
