English

The village now has three free challenges: remember picnic orders, guide a fish through a pearl maze, and light a grove of stars. Each has three difficulty levels and replay variations. Mistakes cost no supplies, and there are no daily limits.

Earn medals to unlock picnic decorations, a swimming fish pond and a starlit village. Switch between earned themes whenever you like. Garden buttons now have distinct animated artwork for planting, cooking, building and neighbours. Reduced-motion settings are respected; existing progress, crop countdowns and combat balance are preserved.

繁體中文

村莊新增三種免費挑戰：記住野餐料理順序、帶小魚收集珍珠並走到終點、解開星光點燈謎題。各有三階難度，重玩時題目會變化；失誤不扣材料，也沒有每日次數限制。

取得徽章可解鎖野餐裝飾、游魚池與星燈夜景，已解鎖主題可隨時切換。種植、料理、建設與居民入口改用各自相關的動態圖示，並支援減少動態設定。既有進度、作物倒數與戰鬥平衡皆保留。
