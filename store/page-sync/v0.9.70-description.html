GOO BLASTER — v0.9.70

A one-thumb survival adventure with soft colours, rounded little monsters, and a Candy Garden to come home to. Drag anywhere to move. Your weapons fire automatically. Dodge, collect upgrades, and find a build that works for you.

  • 50 stages across 10 chapters, with a chapter boss every 5 stages.
  • Choose up to 2 weapons and 4 skills per run. Upgrade your choices and discover weapon evolutions.
  • A permanent Candy Shop: even failed runs earn coins toward upgrades.
  • Candy Village: explore an isometric cottage, fields and pond with walking jelly residents. Drag and zoom the scene, tap a field to plant or harvest, and tap your cottage to restore it. A restoration board shows your next cottage upgrade and guides planting, harvesting and repairs. Six visitor wishes and free decoration tools are in the workshop.
  • Plant seeds and watch the harvest countdown: Mint Sprouts take 1 minute, Berry Blooms 3 minutes, and Moon Blossoms 6 minutes. Crops keep growing offline and never wither. Collect the harvest before moving on to cottage repairs. Clear stages for extra seeds; gardening does not add combat stats.
  • Harvest ingredients, make mint tea, berry jam and moon tarts, and fulfil three neighbours’ orders. Build and improve a sweet shop, picnic pavilion and fountain through six village projects. Orders never expire, recipes are instant, and village reputation does not add combat power.
  • Village adventures: three free challenges with three difficulty levels each. Remember picnic orders, guide a fish to three pearls and a flag, or solve a star-lighting puzzle. Earn medals for picnic, pond and starlight scenery. No supply costs or daily limits; buttons have distinct animated icons and support reduced motion.
  • Bosses have solid bodies. Time your dash carefully; cooldown upgrades are choices you make during each run.
  • 11 languages. No ads, no gacha, and no account required to play. The game works offline after it has loaded.

Controls
Phone: drag to move; use the dash and nuke buttons when available.
Desktop: WASD or arrow keys to move, Space to dash, E for a nuke.

Android test build: Download the current Android test APK. Update the existing app to keep your progress; do not uninstall it.
